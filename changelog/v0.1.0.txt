Let me introduce you to *Grimoire*, my magic implementation.

With a lectern, you can craft your customized colored grimoire
where you will put spell crafted from various ingredient.

You can change selected spell and cast which you want (up to 12).

It's WIP, so the mod only works with commands.
But everything is well handled (I think).

----

/grimoire give -> give a magic grimoire with default spell
/grimoire slot ... -> change selected spell slot
/grimoire cast -> cast the current selected spell slot
/grimoire infuse ... -> update spells (add, remove, purge)
/grimoire check -> show current spells in grimoire

/spell give ... -> give the scroll linked to the spell (Hytale asset logic)
/spell check -> show linked spell of the held scroll
/spell cast ... -> cast spell from scroll or argument

----

For now the only spell I made is fireball.
It's throw a fire projectile that burn mob or nature!

I'm a little proud, because it's not a default behavior of fire projectile in Hytale 🔥🔥🔥
